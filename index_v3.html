XRZENO is a WebAR product-visualization studio in Tangerang Selatan, Banten. We started in early 2026 because no one should have to guess what a product looks like from a flat photo.

So we scan real products and rebuild them as accurate 3D models your customers can turn over, and place in their own space at true size, straight from the browser. No app to install.

We build for everyone, from a single shop to a full catalog, and we keep the process quick and the pricing clear.

How it works

All we need from you is the product.

Discuss

We pick the products with the most to gain from 3D: the ones people want to turn over before they commit. You point them out, we plan the shoot.

Scan

Our team captures the real product in detail, at our studio or on your site.

Build

That capture becomes a clean, accurate 3D model, tuned to load fast on any phone.

Deliver

You get a link and a QR code, ready for a shelf, a table, or your website. Customers point their camera and it's there.

The payoff

People buy what they can see.

They trade up

Let a customer turn your product over at real size and the pricier option stops feeling like a gamble. They reach for it because they already know what they're getting.

No surprises

A customer who has set your product on their own table knows exactly what will arrive. The expectation is set before the sale, so far fewer products get returned.

It spreads

AR is the kind of thing people record and send to a friend. Every share puts your product in front of someone new. Effectively free marketing at no extra cost.
